# AniRoute presentation features

## Crop-aware route planning

- Prefilled 250 kg tomato shipment on a Baguio-to-Calamba corridor.
- Editable crop, quantity, vehicle, pickup, and delivery information.
- Hardcoded tomato, leafy vegetable, fruit, and durable-crop sensitivity profiles.
- Optimal, Safer, and Fastest route choices on one interactive map.
- Numeric Crop Transport Risk Score for every route.
- Visible time, distance, road, flood, weather, and heat factor values.
- Plain-language explanation of why a route is recommended for the crop.
- Crop changes can change the recommended route.

## Active route and road analysis

- Active-trip map, trip estimate, next direction, crop load, and vehicle summary.
- Presentation camera feed that starts without browser permissions.
- Automatic findings for potholes, severe road damage, standing water, and obstructions.
- Confidence, distance, evidence frame, and GPS position in one confirmation dialog.
- Manual and voice confirmation controls.
- Confirmed findings raise the affected route's road factor.
- All route scores are recalculated and a lower-risk alternative is recommended when appropriate.

## Presentation boundaries

- No FastAPI service is needed to run the presentation.
- Route geometry and operating values are illustrative.
- Scores are interpretable transport-risk values, not spoilage percentages or safety guarantees.
- Production road routing, computer vision, weather feeds, and geospatial persistence remain future integrations.
