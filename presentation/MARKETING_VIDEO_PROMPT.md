# AniRoute — 30-second 3D product film prompt (landscape, premium render)

## Purpose to get across

AniRoute is a web app that helps Filipino farmers and produce drivers get perishable harvests to market in
good condition. You enter the crop and the trip, and it plans the route that is **gentlest on the crop**.
During the drive, the phone camera spots **cracked, damaged road** in real time, so the driver can confirm
them and reroute.

**One-line message:** *Protect the harvest on the way to market.*

## Creative direction

**A premium, fully rendered 3D product film, at the level of a flagship tech launch.** Think Apple product
reveals, high-end automotive configurators and Octane, Redshift or Unreal Engine 5 renders. It must not
look like a template, stock animation or flat screen recording.

- **Orientation:** landscape 16:9, rendered at 3840 × 2160 (4K), 30 fps. Deliver 4K and 1080p.
- **Hero:** the **AniRoute interface** is always centred in frame, shown on photoreal 3D devices: a
  thin-bezel laptop and a smartphone in a dashboard mount. Brushed aluminium, glass with real refraction,
  and soft screen glow. The screen content is the real app UI, rendered crisp and legible.
- **World:** a stylised 3D miniature of Philippine terrain.
  - Low-poly-meets-photoreal rice terraces and provincial roads, with volumetric morning haze.
  - The map of the Philippines is **extruded into 3D relief**, in matte green and soft gold materials.
- **Lighting:**
  - Cinematic three-point lighting with a warm golden-hour key light.
  - Cool rim light on the devices.
  - Volumetric light shafts, soft global illumination and subtle bloom on UI elements.
  - Ray-traced reflections on glass and metal.
- **Camera:**
  - One **continuous, flowing camera move** that feels like a single take, gliding between scenes.
  - Virtual lenses: 35 mm for wides, 85 mm for product close-ups.
  - Shallow depth of field with smooth rack focus, and physically correct motion blur.
- **Motion design:**
  - All animation on **smooth ease-in/ease-out curves**, with no linear moves and no snapping.
  - Elements arrive with gentle overshoot and settle.
  - UI panels **lift off the screen into 3D space** as floating glass cards, then dock back in.
- **Transitions:** seamless match-moves and morphs, never cuts or fades to black:
  - A screen **becomes** the map.
  - A route line **becomes** the road.
  - A camera lens **zooms into** the live detection view.
- **Colours:**
  - Base: deep forest green, warm harvest gold, soft off-white.
  - Accents: the app's route colours, which glow as light trails. Recommended is emerald green, Fastest
    amber orange, Safest sapphire blue. Hazard alerts are warm red.
- **Type:** minimal premium sans-serif (SF Pro / Inter Display style), with large tracking and
  kinetic fades. Captions float in 3D space **beside** the hero, never covering the UI.
- **Sound:**
  - Cinematic ambient-electronic score with a soft piano motif. It builds to a lift at about 18 s and
    resolves on the logo.
  - Polished sound design: glassy UI ticks, airy whooshes on camera moves, a soft chime for the alert and
    a low "thrum" when the route locks in.
- **Voice:** warm, confident Filipino-accented narrator, unhurried (English, or Taglish if preferred).

## Single prompt for AI video tools (Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling)

> A premium 30-second landscape 16:9 3D product film for "AniRoute", a web app that helps Filipino farmers

> rendered in Unreal Engine 5 or Octane: ray-traced reflections, volumetric golden-hour light, soft global
> illumination, shallow depth of field and physically correct motion blur. One continuous, flowing camera

## Production notes

- **Real UI on the devices:** composite the **real AniRoute screens** onto the 3D device screens, as
  screen textures or UI panels. Capture them with the example trip: Tomatoes, 250, Baguio City → Calamba,
  Laguna. `presentation/screenshots/` has the Plan, route-option and trip screens; the demo drive video
  gives the detection frames. Keep text sharp: render the UI at 2× and hold each feature at least 2 s.
- **Tools:** Blender (Cycles/EEVEE), Cinema 4D + Redshift or Octane, or Unreal Engine 5. Finish in
  After Effects or DaVinci Resolve with grading, light flares and subtle film grain. Keep motion curves
  smooth in the graph editor: no linear keys.
- **Map accuracy:** the 3D map should follow real Philippine geography, and the route ribbons the real
  Baguio → Calamba roads.
- **Keep it honest:**
  - No accuracy percentages and no promised spoilage reduction: the route scores are heuristics and have
    not been field-tested.
  - The app reliably detects **cracks** (its pothole class needs re-labelled data, see `ml/reports/pothole_label_audit.md`). Don't show it detecting people, animals, floods or
    obstacles.
