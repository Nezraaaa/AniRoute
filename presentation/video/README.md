# AniRoute product film (40 s, 3D)

A rendered 3D marketing film made from the real product: real AniRoute screens captured from the
running app, and the real Baguio City → Calamba routes from the AniRoute backend drawn on a 3D relief
map of Luzon. It is rendered in Three.js on the GPU, frame by frame, and encoded with ffmpeg. It
follows the brief in [`../MARKETING_VIDEO_PROMPT.md`](../MARKETING_VIDEO_PROMPT.md).

Output: `out/AniRoute_ProductFilm_1080p.mp4` (1920 × 1080, 60 fps, 40 s, H.264 + AAC). A smaller copy for sharing is
`out/AniRoute_ProductFilm_1080p_web.mp4`.

**Tagalog version:** `out/AniRoute_ProductFilm_1080p_Tagalog.mp4` (and `_Tagalog_web.mp4`). It has a male
Tagalog narrator (Microsoft neural voice *Angelo*, fil-PH), with Tagalog captions and tagline. The app
screens stay in English, as the app itself is.
Build it with `python make_narration.py --lang tl`, `python make_audio.py --lang tl` and
`node render.mjs --lang tl`.

## Story (one continuous camera)

| Time | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Golden hour on a hilltop farm: a farmer in a salakot carries a crate of tomatoes and loads it onto the pickup. |
| 6–9 s | The camera glides to the farm table, where a laptop is running AniRoute. |
| 9–14 s | The **delivery form** lifts off the screen as a glass card and fills in (Tomatoes, 250 kg, Baguio City → Calamba). |
| 14–17 s | A **gold guide line** leaps out of the route on the laptop screen. The camera follows it over the farm and the valley; the 3D map of Luzon unfolds ahead and the line lands on **Baguio**. |
| 17–23 s | The mountains rise and the three real routes ignite from Baguio (Recommended green, Fastest orange, Safest blue). Each route card stands on **its own road**, with a leader line. |
| 23–28 s | The camera dives along the recommended road, which becomes a real road; the farmer is now driving the pickup. |
| 28–33 s | Over the tomato crates, through the cab's rear window and past the driver, to the dashboard phone running the **Trip screen**. The detector boxes **severe road damage**, and the hazard dialog floats out. |
| 33–37 s | Tap **Confirm**. The camera pushes through the dialog into the map, the hazard pin drops and the routes re-score. |
| 37–40 s | The route ribbons rise into the **AniRoute** logo: *Protect the harvest on the way to market.* |

**Narration:** a female voice, Microsoft neural voice *Rosa* (en-PH), generated by `make_narration.py`
through `edge-tts`. Only the nine narration lines are sent to the speech service. The script:

> Every harvest deserves to arrive fresh. · Tell AniRoute what you're carrying, and where it's going. ·
> It finds the real roads to your market, and scores each one for time, crop, and road safety. ·
> Choose the gentlest road for your harvest. · Then follow it, every kilometer of the way. ·
> Your phone's camera spots cracked, damaged road, in real time. · Confirm with a tap, and AniRoute finds a
> safer way. · Protect the harvest, on the way to market.

**Music and sound design:** original, synthesised by `make_audio.py`, with no samples or licensed music.
The music ducks under the voice.

**Motion:**
- One continuous camera path, with keyframes that never reverse mid-move, plus Gaussian temporal
  smoothing.
- After the 6 s opening, the story plays at about 0.82× speed, so every line has room to breathe.
- The gold guide line motivates the laptop-to-map move. The camera keeps the screen in frame at the
  launch and stays below about 50°/s of rotation. Both sides of the phone-to-map dissolve keep moving
  forward.
- Rendered at 60 fps, with 4 motion-blur sub-frames per frame.
- Real motion blur: 5 sub-frames per frame, 180° shutter.
- Warm light-leak sweeps on each transition.
- Golden dust motes in the sunlight.

## Re-render

Requirements:
- Node 18+ and Chrome or Edge.
- `ffmpeg` on PATH.
- Python with numpy and scipy.
- An internet connection, for Google Fonts and the narration voice.
- `pip install edge-tts`.

```bash
cd presentation/video
npm install                       # three, topojson-client, world-atlas
node prepare-geo.mjs              # Philippine coastline (Natural Earth 1:10m) -> assets/philippines.json
node capture-assets.mjs           # needs backend :8000 + frontend :5173 running; real UI + routes -> assets/
python make_narration.py          # female voice-over (needs internet) -> assets/narration/
python make_audio.py              # music + sound design + voice, ducked -> assets/soundtrack.wav
node render.mjs                   # -> out/AniRoute_ProductFilm_1080p.mp4  60 fps (about 22 min on a mid-range GPU)
node render.mjs --fps 30          # 30 fps version (about 13 min)
node render.mjs --4k              # 3840 x 2160 version
node render.mjs --stills 4.4,12.5 # quick look at chosen seconds -> out/stills/
```

To preview it live, serve this folder (any static server) and open `scene.html`. Add `?t=12.5` to
freeze on a moment.

## Honesty notes

- Every screen shown is a capture of the real app.
- The detection frame is the deployed detector's own output on a held-out RDD2022 test image.
- No accuracy numbers or loss-reduction claims appear in the film.
- One line is hidden on the hazard dialog: "No microphone was found" appears only because the
  headless capture browser has no microphone.
